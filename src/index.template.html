Step 1Relay for internet connections

Between two homes, phones or countries, a direct connection fails more often than it works: mobile data and many Wi-Fi networks block it. A free relay makes it work on any network and hides your IP address. You set it up once; only the person who starts the session needs one.

How to get a free relay (about 2 minutes)
  1. Create a free account with a TURN provider, for example ExpressTURN or Metered Open Relay.
  2. In its dashboard, copy the TURN server address (for example free.expressturn.com:3478), the username and the password. You don't need a "secret key".
  3. Paste them below, exactly as shown. If the provider lists several addresses, paste them all, one per line.
  4. Click Test relay.
Temporary logins (stronger protection when sharing)

If your relay supports temporary logins (the "TURN REST API": for example ExpressTURN's Premium "Secret Key", or your own coturn server), paste its secret key here. Invites then carry a login that the relay itself rejects once the time below runs out, and your password and secret key never leave your device. You can leave the password empty if you use this.
